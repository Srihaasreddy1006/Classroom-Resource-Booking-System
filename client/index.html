Conflict-Free Engine
Core Technical Features

Designed for Zero Conflicting Bookings

Built with an automated double-booking prevention engine, role-based access control, and complete audit logging on MongoDB.

Conflict Prevention Engine

Server-side interval overlap detection (S1 < E2 && E1 > S2) ensures no two approved bookings ever collide for the same classroom or equipment.

Portable Equipment Allocation

Dynamic tracking of projectors, smart boards, microphones, and lab kits. Inventories are decremented on request and restored on cancellation or rejection.

Role-Based Access Control

Tailored dashboards for students (request and track), faculty (view schedules and equipment), and administrators (approve, reject, manage facilities).
